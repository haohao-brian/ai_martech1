## Executive Summary

This audit evaluates all 67 Meta-Principles (MPs) within their chapter context, recognizing that constitutional-level principles can vary in abstraction based on their domain. The analysis follows the ROC Constitution analogy where different chapters address different aspects of governance while maintaining constitutional authority.
